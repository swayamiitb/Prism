# Pricing Exceptions

> **Owner:** Sales Operations (Lead: Devon Park)
> **Applies to:** Discounts, credits, and custom pricing for existing deals

## Standard discounts

Account Executives can apply up to **15% discount** on a deal without approval.
Anything above 15% requires Sales Manager approval; above 25% requires VP Sales
approval.

## Credits

Goodwill credits under $200 can be issued by any AE. Above $200, escalate to
Sales Ops. Credits are applied in Salesforce and synced to Billing.

## Custom contracts

Custom pricing (non-standard SKUs, multi-year commitments) must go through
Deal Desk. Submit via the Deal Desk Jira queue with the term sheet attached.
Standard turnaround is 2 business days.

## Hard rules

- Never backdate a pricing change.
- Never offer equity-style discounts ("lifetime deal") without written VP approval.
- All exceptions are logged in Salesforce for the quarterly review.
