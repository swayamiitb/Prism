# Refund Policy

> **Owner:** Support team (Lead: Maya Chen)
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-15
> **Applies to:** All customer-initiated refunds

## Principles

1. We want refunds to be fast and painless for legitimate cases.
2. We protect against fraud and abuse.
3. Every refund is logged in Zendesk for audit.

## Standard refunds (under $500)

Any Support Agent can issue a standard refund without approval. The original
payment method must be used (via Stripe). Log the refund in the Zendesk ticket
with the reason code.

## Large refunds ($500 or more)

Large refunds **require escalation to the Billing Lead** (currently Maya Chen)
before issuing. The Billing Lead verifies the charge in Stripe, checks the
account history for repeated refunds, and approves or denies. Once approved,
the Support Agent issues the refund via Stripe and logs it.

## Hard limit

**Never issue a refund greater than 2x the original charge amount.** If a case
seems to require more, escalate to the Head of Finance.
